Key takeaways

  • A social casino uses free virtual coins with no cash value.
  • A sweepstakes casino adds a free-entry sweepstakes so eligible players can redeem certain prizes.
  • A real-money casino takes cash bets and needs a state gambling licence.

What "sweepstakes" really means

A sweepstakes is a promotion with a prize. To stay a sweepstakes and not a lottery, one rule is essential: no purchase is necessary. That is why a sweepstakes casino must offer a free way to take part, often called an alternative method of entry (AMOE).

  • You can get free game credit without buying anything.
  • Buying extra credit is optional and never improves your odds.
  • Prizes are redeemed under published official rules.

Two balances, two jobs

Most sweepstakes sites keep two separate balances. One is for fun play only and has no cash value. The other comes from the sweepstakes itself, and amounts in it can be redeemed for prizes once you meet the official rules, for example verification, any play-through requirement and the minimum redemption amount.

Names vary from site to site, so do not assume one site works like another. In your MonsterSweeps account, the balance screen shows which amounts are eligible to redeem.

Read the play-through and minimum rules first. Knowing them up front saves the most common surprise.

Redeeming is not the same as withdrawing

At a real-money casino you withdraw your own cash. At a sweepstakes casino you redeem a prize you won in the promotion. The difference sounds small, but it explains the extra steps:

  1. Verify your identity so the prize reaches the right adult.
  2. Meet the rules for the amount you want to redeem.
  3. Request the redemption to a wallet in your own name.

A quick word on taxes

In the US, prizes are generally treated as taxable income, and sweepstakes prizes are no exception. Where the law requires it, an operator may ask for your taxpayer information and send you a tax form.

Keep a simple record of what you redeem. We are not tax advisers, so if you are unsure what applies to you, ask a qualified tax professional.

Four myths, busted

"Buying coins improves my chances"

No. Purchases are optional and never change your odds.

"This game is due to pay out"

No. Every result is independent. Past spins do not affect the next one.

"Sweepstakes sites have no rules"

They must follow sweepstakes, promotion and consumer protection laws, plus any state rules.

"If it is online, it is legal everywhere"

No. Availability depends on where you live, and it is changing.

How to choose a site safely

Whatever you play on, check these before you sign up:

  • Published rules and a clear free way to take part.
  • Real age and ID checks, not just a tick-box.
  • Responsible play tools such as limits, time-outs and self-exclusion.
  • Clear redemption terms and a real support contact.
Red flags

Promises of guaranteed wins, pressure to buy more, requests for your wallet PIN or one-time codes, or no contact details at all. Walk away.

Rules are changing

Sweepstakes casino rules vary by state, and several states have been tightening them. Eligibility is checked when you sign up. Only play where you are eligible, and never use a VPN or proxy to get around a location check. It can get your account closed.

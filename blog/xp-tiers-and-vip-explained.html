XP Tiers and the VIP Club, Explained

Key takeaways

  • XP lifts you through five tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond.
  • Every tier keeps all the benefits of the tier below it.
  • The free Fortune Wheel and referral bonuses earn XP too. You never have to deposit to climb.
  • VIP is by invitation only. Diamond players are offered a place.

What XP is

XP is the number that measures your progress. You start at Bronze with 0 XP, and as your XP grows you move up the tiers automatically.

What earns XP

  • Deposits
  • Referral bonuses
  • Fortune Wheel spins
  • Your sign-up bonus

What does not

Birthday bonuses, weekly reward percentages and VIP offers are paid as game credit only. They do not add XP.

The Fortune Wheel: free XP every day

Every player, on every tier, gets one free Fortune Wheel spin every 24 hours. Prizes include XP, free play and vouchers, and no purchase is needed.

It is the simplest way to climb. A spin a day adds up over a month, and it costs nothing. If you like reminders, set one at a time of day that suits you, then close the app when you are done.

How progress works

  • XP only goes up as you earn it. You move to the next tier automatically the moment you cross its threshold.
  • Nothing is lost when you climb. Each tier keeps every benefit of the tiers below it.
  • XP is a loyalty score, not money. It cannot be redeemed, sold or transferred.
  • Credit-only rewards do not add XP. Birthday bonuses, weekly rewards and VIP offers are paid as game credit.

Your account shows your current XP and how far you are from the next tier.

The VIP Club

The VIP Club is invitation only. Players who reach Diamond are offered a place, and we may also invite players directly. There is no application form and no fee. VIP members also get a bigger referral reward: 80% as credit and 80% as XP of a friend's first deposit, versus 50% for standard members.

Explore the VIP Club or see the referral rules.

The referral boost

Inviting friends you know is another way to earn XP. When a friend you invite makes their first deposit, you receive a share of it as game credit and the same share as XP:

  • Standard members: 50% as credit and 50% as XP.
  • VIP members: 80% as credit and 80% as XP.

Your friend must be new to MonsterSweeps, 21 or older, verified, and must enter your code when they sign up. Read the full rules.

Climb without overspending

  • Claim your free daily spin. It earns XP at no cost.

  • Set a purchase limit before you deposit, and treat deposits as entertainment spending.
Tiers are a bonus, not a goal

Never spend more than you can afford to move up. Responsible gaming tools are free.

Quick answers

How do I earn XP?

XP comes from deposits, referral bonuses, Fortune Wheel spins and your sign-up bonus. Birthday bonuses, weekly rewards and VIP offers are game credit only and do not add XP.

Do I lose tier benefits if I do not play for a while?

Your account shows your current tier, your XP and the rules on how long XP and tiers last. If anything is unclear, email support@monstersweeps.net and we will explain it.

How do I join the VIP Club?

VIP is by invitation only. Players who reach Diamond (2,000 XP) are offered a place, and we may also invite players directly. There is no application form and no fee.

Do I need to deposit to climb?

No. The free Fortune Wheel and referral bonuses also earn XP. Deposits are optional and never required to move up.

2Information We Collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Account data: full name, username, email address, phone number, date of birth, state of residence, password (stored hashed), and security answers.
  • Verification data: government-issued ID, selfie or liveness image, proof of address, and, where required for prize redemption or tax reporting, a Social Security number or taxpayer ID.
  • Payment and redemption data: see Section 4.
  • Communications: support tickets, live-chat transcripts, complaints, survey answers, and referral or promotion entries.
  • User content: anything you post in chat, comments or public areas, which other users may see.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and log data: IP address, device type and identifiers, operating system, browser type, language, time zone, crash and diagnostic logs.
  • Usage and gameplay data: pages and games viewed, spins, wagers of virtual currency, wins, balances, session length, frequency, how you heard about us, and your interactions with promotions and ads.
  • Location data: approximate or precise location derived from your IP address or device, used to confirm you are in an eligible state and to prevent fraud.
  • Cookies, pixels, tags and SDKs: see Section 8.

2.3 Inferences

We may draw inferences from the data above (for example, preferences, risk signals, or likelihood of fraud) to personalize the Services and protect the platform.

3Where We Get Your Information

  • Directly from you when you register, verify, play, buy virtual currency, redeem, or contact us.
  • Automatically from your browser or device when you use the Services.
  • Digital wallet providers (such as Chime, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, Google Pay and Apple Pay), who confirm that a payment or payout succeeded and return limited details.
  • Identity, fraud and sanctions-screening vendors, who return verification and risk results.
  • Social sign-in and referral partners, if you register through a third-party account or a referral link.
  • Advertising and analytics partners, who tell us which campaigns brought you to us.

4Payments, Wallets & Redemptions

Purchases of optional virtual currency and prize redemptions are processed through MonsterPay, our own payment system, together with the digital wallet services you choose, which may include Chime, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, Google Pay and Apple Pay.

  • What MonsterPay processes: your wallet handle or tag (for example a Cash App $Cashtag or Venmo username), the name and email or phone tied to the wallet, transaction ID, amount, status and time, and the payment method type. This information is treated as personal information under this Policy.
  • What wallet providers handle: your wallet or bank credentials, authentication and the movement of funds. Those providers process this data under their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them. We never receive your wallet password, PIN or one-time passcode.
  • Name matching: to protect against fraud and money laundering, we may require that the name on a wallet or bank account matches the verified name on your MonsterSweeps account before we send a prize.
  • Tax reporting: where IRS rules require it, we collect the taxpayer information needed to report prize redemptions and may share it with the IRS and state tax authorities.
  • Fraud and chargeback prevention: we and the wallet providers may share transaction and device signals to detect stolen payment methods, chargeback abuse and multi-accounting.
We never ask for your wallet password, PIN or one-time passcode. If someone claiming to be MonsterSweeps asks for them, do not respond and contact us.

5Identity Verification & Biometric Data

To confirm you are 21 or older, one person per account, and eligible to redeem prizes, we use third-party identity verification providers. You may be asked to submit a photo ID and a selfie or short liveness video. The provider compares your selfie to your ID photo using facial recognition, which creates a face template (biometric data) and returns a pass/fail result to us.

  • We collect biometric data only for identity verification and fraud prevention, and only with your consent where state law requires it.
  • The verification provider stores biometric data under its own policy and a contract with us. We keep the verification result and, where applicable, related biometric data only until the purpose is met or [3] years after your last interaction with us, whichever comes first, unless a longer period is legally required.
  • We do not sell, lease or trade biometric data.
  • You may decline biometric verification, but we may then be unable to verify your account or process redemptions.

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share it with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose it only in these situations:

18Rule Violations, Account Actions & Disputes

You must follow our Terms of Use and Official Sweepstakes Rules. If we find a violation, such as multiple accounts, false identity information, payment fraud or chargeback abuse, bonus abuse, collusion, bots or location spoofing, we may warn you, restrict features, suspend or permanently close your account, and remove you from the Services.

  • One account per person: each player may have only one account. To enforce this we compare account details, verified ID, device information and IP address. If we find multiple accounts, or an account used by or shared with someone else, we may close all the accounts involved and remove the player from the Services. See our Responsible Gaming page for how we handle shared homes and networks.
  • One dispute per action: if you disagree with an account action, you may submit one dispute for that action, with any evidence you have, to [support email]. We will review it and reply in writing.
  • Repeat disputes: further disputes about the same action may be declined. Repeated or abusive use of the dispute process may lead to permanent removal from the Services.
  • Our right to remove players: we may remove any player who breaks our rules, and may refuse to re-register a player who was removed.
  • Ban-evasion and fraud records: after an account is closed, we may keep identifiers such as your name, ID verification result, device and IP data and wallet handles, so that removed players cannot return and to prevent fraud (see Section 9).
  • Your privacy rights are separate: the one-dispute rule applies to account decisions only. It does not limit the privacy rights and appeal process described in Section 11.
What happens to balances and prizes when an account is closed is set out in the Terms of Use and Official Sweepstakes Rules.

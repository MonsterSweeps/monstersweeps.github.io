4Rules & Eligibility

  • 21+ only. You and your friend must be at least 21, live in an eligible US state, and have a verified account.
  • New players only. Your friend must be a new customer who has never had a MonsterSweeps account.
  • One account per person. No self-referrals, duplicate accounts or accounts shared with others. We check account details, verified ID, device information and IP address, and referrals between accounts that match may not qualify.
  • Code at sign-up. Your friend must enter your code during registration. It cannot be added afterwards.
  • Qualifying deposit. Rewards are credited after your friend's first deposit is successfully completed and their account is verified. Reversed, refunded or charged-back deposits do not qualify, and any rewards paid on them may be removed.
  • Limits. Minimum first deposit: [$ amount]. Maximum rewarded referrals: [number, or "no limit"].
  • No spam or misleading promotion. Share honestly with people you know. Do not post your code on coupon sites, use bots, mass-message strangers, or claim guaranteed winnings.
  • Abuse has consequences. If we find fraud or rule-breaking, we may cancel rewards, close the accounts involved and remove the players, as described in our Terms of Use.
  • Changes. We may change, pause or end the referral program at any time. Rewards already earned before a change are honored.

Referral rewards are part of our promotions. They do not change the fact that a sweepstakes entry method with no purchase necessary is always available. See the Official Sweepstakes Rules.

5Sharing Tips

  • Tell friends and family you actually know and think will enjoy it.
  • Send your code in a personal message or your own social posts, and be clear it is for adults 21+.
  • Explain that play is for entertainment, that results are random, and that they should only play within their means.
  • Never promise winnings, and never pressure anyone to deposit.

6FAQ

When do I get my reward?

Right after your friend's first deposit is confirmed and their account is verified. You will see it in your balance and XP.

Is there a limit to how many friends I can refer?

Maximum rewarded referrals: [number, or "no limit"].

My friend forgot to enter my code. Can we add it later?

No. The code must be entered during registration. Ask your friend to contact support before making a deposit if there was a mistake.

Can I refer someone in my household?

Yes, if they are a different verified adult with their own account. Because accounts that share a device or network are reviewed, tell support at [support email] so we can check it properly.

Do my rewards expire?

Game credit and XP follow the terms stated in your account and in the Terms of Use.

What happens if my friend's deposit is reversed?

The referral no longer qualifies, and any rewards already paid for it may be taken back.

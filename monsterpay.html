How it's structured

Three levels, one set of rails

Every level sees its own book and nothing above it. A merchant never sees us at all if their partner doesn't want them to.

LEVEL 01

Reseller

You, under your own brand

  • Your brand name, logo, colour and subdomain
  • Onboard and price your own merchants
  • Commission paid into your wallet
  • No integration, no developers
LEVEL 02

Merchant

The business taking payments

  • Payment links or the full API
  • Branded checkout for their customers
  • Settlements, refunds and disputes
  • Dashboard scoped to their own data
Planned LEVEL 03

Player

The person paying

  • Pays in from their phone
  • Cashes out to their own wallet
  • Messages the merchant directly
  • Three screens, nothing to learn
Platform

What you actually get

Not a payment page with your logo on it. Pick the side you're coming in on.

Payment links or full API

Spin up a shareable link in seconds with no website, or wire the API into your platform and create an order per transaction.

Automatic, with a brake

Transactions clear automatically, with manual override and configurable limits wherever you want a human in the loop.

Summary reporting

All-time and monthly totals for purchases, withdrawals, fees owed, disputes and refunds, and net cash flow.

Role-based staff limits

Approval ceilings set per role, so a junior cashier and a senior manager don't carry the same authority.

Settlements and refunds

A settlement cycle you can see, refunds handled in the dashboard, and disputes flagged with the action window attached.

Alerts that matter

Email and SMS on failed payments, refunds, settlements, disputes and webhook failures, plus a high-value alert you set.

# No code at all: create a link in the dashboard,
# or from the API if you prefer.

curl https://api.monsterpay.net/v1/links \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MONSTERPAY_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \

Questions

Before you ask

Which payment methods are live right now?

Customers pay in with Google Pay, Apple Pay or Chime. You take settlement out to an ACH bank account, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, Chime or a debit card. Crypto is on the roadmap and is not live yet — we'd rather say that than list it and make you chase it.

Do I need a website or developers to start?

Neither. Create a payment link with a title and an amount, share it, and you're collecting. For resellers we build the branded platform and hand you the keys. API keys, webhooks and documentation come later, if and when you want to build your own gateway on top.

Why are the checkout amounts fixed?

The checkout accepts a set list of amounts, from $9.99 up to $199.99. That's a constraint of the rails we run on today, not a design choice, and it's the honest answer. If your business needs arbitrary amounts or larger single transactions, say so early and we'll tell you straight whether we fit.

Can the platform carry my branding?

Depends which level you come in at. Merchants get their logo and accent colour on the checkout their customers see. Resellers get the lot — brand name, logo, colour, their own address at yourcompany.monsterpay.net, their own support email, and the option to switch off any mention of MonsterPay on the panel and the checkout.

How long does onboarding take?

Fast, because there's nothing for you to configure. We set up your branded platform at yourcompany.monsterpay.net and hand you the login — no domain purchase, no DNS, no certificate. A merchant onboarded under an existing reseller takes about a minute.

What do I need ready before I contact you?

Your business and vertical, your expected monthly volume, which methods you need, and whether you're processing yourself or reselling. That's enough to get a rate and a timeline back.

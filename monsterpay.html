Who it's for

Two ways to work with MonsterPay

Run your own volume through it, or resell it. The platform is the same; the economics are not.

Payment links or full API

Spin up a shareable payment link in seconds with no website required, or wire the API into your platform and create an order per transaction. Same methods either way.

Your own scoped dashboard

Log in and see only your account: deposits, withdrawals, fees owed, disputes and refunds, all-time and by month.

Automatic, with a hand on the brake

Deposits and withdrawals clear automatically through the API, with manual override and limits where you want oversight.

Staff limits that match the role

Role-based tiers rather than one flat ceiling. Junior staff and senior staff get different approval limits.

Checkout in your colours

Your logo and your accent colour on the checkout your customers see. Test mode and live mode side by side, so you can prove the flow before a cent moves.

One point of contact

You deal with us. Not with a chain of upstream processors you've never spoken to.

It's your gateway, not ours

Your brand name, your logo, your colour, your domain. Switch off "Powered by MonsterPay" and nothing on the panel or the checkout says we exist.

Your own domain

Your panel and your checkout run on a domain you choose. We activate it, you publish the DNS records, and your merchants never see another name.

You set each merchant's price

Your commission rate is set per merchant, not fixed across the book. Price the ones you fought for differently from the ones that walked in.

Onboard a merchant in a minute

Four steps — business, contact, pricing, review — and the owner login goes out. No per-merchant build, no engineering ticket.

A wallet you control

Commission moves to your available balance as your merchants' settlements are paid. Withdraw on request, or set rules and let it run.

Tiers that pay you more

Partners move up through tiers as volume grows, and the rate moves with them. The book you build is the rate you get.

How it's structured

Four levels, one set of rails

Each level sees its own book and nothing above it. A merchant never sees us at all if their partner doesn't want them to.

Level 1

MonsterPay HQ

Gateway routing, settlement, commissions, KYC review and audit across the whole network. Ours alone.

Level 2

Reseller

A fully white-labelled platform — own brand, own domain, own checkout, own support desk. Onboards and prices its own merchants and draws commission to its wallet.

Level 3

Merchant

Payment links or the API, transactions, refunds, settlements and payout accounts. Branded checkout, scoped to their own data.

Level 4 · planned

Player accounts

An optional layer beneath a merchant, kept deliberately plain: pay in, get paid out, message the merchant. Nothing else on the screen.

Questions

Before you ask

Which payment methods are live right now?

Customers pay in with Google Pay, Apple Pay or Chime. You take settlement out to an ACH bank account, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, Chime or a debit card. Crypto is on the roadmap and is not live yet — we'd rather say that than list it and make you chase it.

Do I need a website to start taking payments?

No. Create a payment link with a title and an amount, share it, and you're collecting. The API is there when you want payments inside your own product instead.

Why are the checkout amounts fixed?

The checkout accepts a set list of amounts, from $9.99 up to $199.99. That's a constraint of the rails we run on today, not a design choice, and it's the honest answer. If your business needs arbitrary amounts or larger single transactions, say so early and we'll tell you straight whether we fit.

Can the platform carry my branding?

Depends which level you come in at. Merchants get their logo and accent colour on the checkout their customers see. Resellers get the lot — brand name, logo, colour, their own domain, their own support email, and the option to switch off any mention of MonsterPay entirely.

How long does onboarding take?

The account and key issuance are handled by us and are usually quick. The variable is your side: whitelisting the right server IP and wiring the API into your platform.

Do I need developers to get started?

No. We build your branded platform and hand you the keys. You create merchants and players from your own panel, and nothing needs integrating. API keys, webhooks and documentation come later, if and when you want to build your own gateway on top.
